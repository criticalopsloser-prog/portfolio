About

I'm a 3D artist and illustrator working across product visualization, abstract sculptural studies, and character-driven illustration. My process usually starts in the viewport — blocking form and light before anything else — and moves toward texture, material, and mood last.

Previously worked with studios and independent clients on campaign visuals, motion stills, and editorial illustration. Based between screens, mostly awake at night.
